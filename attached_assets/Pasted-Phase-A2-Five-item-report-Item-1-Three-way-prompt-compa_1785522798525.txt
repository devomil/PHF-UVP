Item 3 — Sound design timeout (report only)
generateProjectSoundDesign (sound-design-service.ts:113) is a sequential for loop — one scene at a time, no parallelism, no per-call timeout. Per scene: up to 4 serial ElevenLabs SFX API calls (transition-in, transition-out, ambient, optional emphasis).

From the smoke test: 2-scene project timed out after ~120 s in the SFX phase. That puts the per-scene ceiling at ~60 s, or ~15 s per ElevenLabs call. Scaling:

Scenes	Estimated API calls	Estimated wall-clock
2	~6	~90 s
5	~15	~225 s (⚠ near timeout)
10	~30	~450 s (☠ always fails)
Nothing to fix per scope — reporting the structural issue: sequential design means sound design scales linearly with scene count and will time out on any project over ~4 scenes. The fix would be parallelizing generateProjectSoundDesign to run scenes concurrently (analogous to how parallel video tasks work).
